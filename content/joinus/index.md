## Prospective Graduate Students (Ph.D. / M.S.)
We are seeking highly motivated Ph.D. and M.S. students with strong backgrounds in robotics, mechanical engineering, control systems, or biomedical engineering. 

If you are interested in joining the lab, please send an email to **Dr. Artemiadis** with:
* Your CV/Resume
* Transcripts (unofficial)
* A brief statement of research interests and relevant experience

---

## Postdoctoral Researchers
Postdoctoral positions become available periodically depending on research funding. Qualified candidates with expertise in robotics, control, machine learning, signal processing or other related fields are encouraged to reach out directly.

---

## Undergraduate Researchers
UD undergraduate students interested in gaining hands-on research experience in human-robot interaction, rehabilitation engineering and related fields should contact us with their resume and a brief statement of research interests and relevant experience.
