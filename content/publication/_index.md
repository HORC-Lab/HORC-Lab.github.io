*Note for: Chuong H Nguyen, George K Karavas and Panagiotis Artemiadis, “Inferring imagined speech using EEG signals: a new approach using Riemannian manifold features,” Journal of Neural Engineering, 15.1, 016002, 2018.
<br><br>
The channels in this study were arranged according to the right diagram in this <a href="/images/actiCAP_vs_actiCHamp_128Ch.jpg" download="actiCAP_vs_actiCHamp_128Ch.jpg">[picture]</a>. E.g. Fp1 and Fp2 should be 1 and 32 green, respectively. Specifically, channels 1-32 were at 1-32 of Green, and 33-64 were at 1-32 Yellow. We then took 4 channels out and placed them around the eyes to record EOG. We used 1 and 10 green for the VOG (vertical oculographic artifact) and 1 and 32 yellow for the HOG (horizontal oculographic artifact). So if you have 64 channels you use [2-9], [11-32], [34-63] for the true signal.
